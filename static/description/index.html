Product Cost from Purchase Orders

Your product cost follows the price you actually pay. Every confirmed purchase order updates it automatically.

The problem

Margins on quotations, sales orders and reports are computed from the product Cost field. Prices from suppliers change all the time, but nobody remembers to update the cost by hand, so the margins you look at are fiction.

What you get

Automatic

The cost is updated the moment a purchase order is confirmed - also after a manager approval.

Real net price

Discounts are taken into account, taxes are not. Converted to the product unit of measure and the company currency.

Several lines, one product

When a product appears on several lines of one order, the cost is the quantity-weighted average.

Traceable

The product shows which order set its cost, when, and the original price and currency. Every change is logged in the chatter.

Safe for valuation

FIFO and Average Cost products are skipped - Odoo updates them on receipt. Free lines never wipe the cost.

Per company

Switch it on or off for each company in the Purchase settings.

How to use it

1
Install the app. The option Update Product Cost from Purchase Orders in Purchase > Configuration > Settings is on by default.
2
Make sure your products use the Standard Price costing method (product category, Inventory Valuation section - visible with the Inventory app).
3
Confirm a purchase order. The Cost of its products is updated.
4
Open a product: the Purchase tab shows the order that set the cost, the date and the price paid; the chatter keeps the history.

Good to know

  • With automated inventory valuation, a cost change on a product in stock creates the usual revaluation entry, exactly as a manual change of the cost would.
  • Lines with a zero price (free goods, samples) are ignored.
  • Cancelling a purchase order does not restore the previous cost.
  • The trace fields are kept per company and per product variant.
